## Flagged for caution
- **Direction of the "cheating" accusation**: early wire summaries were ambiguous about who
  accused whom. The Scottish Daily Express's detailed account (source #5) clarifies: Darrel
  accused Walter of infidelity after finding a text on his phone reading "I guess you are having
  fun in Bothwell without your wife, cheater" — meaning Darrel confronted Walter about his own
  apparent cheating, not the reverse. Script.md must get this direction right.
- **Buchanan's age discrepancy**: one summarized source listed him as "37," which is almost
  certainly a data-extraction error (confusing his age with Darrel's) — the official Judiciary
  of Scotland opinion and every other direct-fetched source consistently give his age as 66.
  Treat "66" as correct.
- **Racist text message**: a real, reported fact ("I hope you die you black b****," found on
  Darrel's phone, sent by Buchanan) — factual and directly relevant to the case (aggravating
  context for the relationship's volatility), not gratuitous; include factually, without dwelling.
- **Background bystanders in Darrel's personal photo** (source photo, real, non-mugshot):
  multiple other diners are visible in the original frame. Cropped to remove the most
  identifiable one (a child) before use; Darrel's own eyes are redacted per the channel's
  standing photo policy — see `PersonPhotos.md`.
