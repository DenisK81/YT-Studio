## Flagged for caution
- **"Two of Lee Eun-hae's previous romantic partners died before Yoon, one with an insurance
  payout"**: reported by Koreaboo and referenced in one other search summary, but with no names,
  dates, or circumstances given anywhere found. A separate summary explicitly noted police
  investigated rumors of these deaths (a 2014 Thailand drowning and a car accident) and found
  "insufficient evidence for criminal charges." Scripted only as an investigated-but-unproven
  rumor, never as a confirmed fact or an implied "serial killer" pattern.
- **Exact start date of the Lee/Cho affair**: sources agree it was "years-long" and ongoing well
  before the murder, but no outlet gives an exact start date. Script will say "years" rather than
  asserting a specific year.
- **"Murder by omission" legal theory**: all three courts (Incheon District, Seoul High Court,
  Supreme Court) explicitly rejected the prosecution's theory that Lee/Cho directly pushed or
  struck Yoon, convicting instead on the theory that they deliberately failed to rescue him while
  he drowned, despite being able to. This is a real, specific, and legally significant distinction
  — the script must not describe a direct physical assault into the water, only the failure to
  help him surface/breathe once he was in genuine distress, consistent with what all three courts
  actually found.
- **Yoon's exact savings figures**: sources give a few different large-number estimates (300
  million won at marriage, 600-700 million won allegedly misappropriated by the family's later
  civil claim, 800 million won in insurance policies) — these are three different figures for
  three different things, not interchangeable; script will keep each precisely attached to what
  it actually describes.
