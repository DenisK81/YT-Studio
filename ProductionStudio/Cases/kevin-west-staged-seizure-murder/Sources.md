in kind to how court-filing-sourced local reporting served as a strong source in prior cases.

## Access method note (technical)
`WebFetch` succeeded directly on Camas-Washougal Post-Record and KPTV (no blocking), but
returned HTTP 403 on every Columbian.com URL and Court TV — consistent with this session's
recurring pattern of certain local-news CMS platforms blocking automated fetches. Worked around
via `WebSearch`'s own content summarization for those, cross-checked for quote/fact consistency
across at least 2 other independent outlets before treating anything from a blocked source as
verified.

## Flagged for caution
- **"Rear naked chokehold" detail**: reported as something prosecutors alleged and West denied
  in his testimony — not an independently confirmed physical fact, but a specific characterization
  used at trial. Scripted only as "prosecutors alleged," never as a settled fact beyond what the
  medical examiner's own findings (bruising, hemorrhaging, asphyxia ruling) independently support.
- **Exact marriage length**: West's own text ("unhappy for 23 years") implies a ~23-year
  marriage; no source gives an explicit wedding date, so the script uses "over two decades"
  rather than asserting an exact anniversary.
- **Cynthia Ward's full current relationship status / any other charges**: no source suggests
  she was charged with anything; she is referenced only as the other party in the affair,
  consistent with how this channel doesn't speculate about non-defendant private individuals
  beyond what's factually reported.
